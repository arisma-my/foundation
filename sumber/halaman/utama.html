[[Mengenai ARISMA Foundation||About ARISMA Foundation]]

[[Visi||Vision]]

[[Menjadi peneraju utama dalam memperkasa sosioekonomi dan kebajikan komuniti autisme serta golongan memerlukan.||To be a leading force in empowering the socio-economic wellbeing and welfare of the autism community and those in need.]]

[[Misi||Mission]]

[[Membangunkan program kesedaran, program latihan, serta jaringan sokongan holistik berasaskan nilai-nilai murni.||Developing awareness programmes, training programmes and a holistic support network built on sound values.]]

[[Objektif utama||Key objectives]]

  • [[Platform sokongan komuniti & pembangunan modal insan.||A community support platform and human-capital development.]]
  • [[Pengurusan dana infaq dan kebajikan yang telus, beretika dan berimpak tinggi.||Transparent, ethical and high-impact management of giving and welfare funds.]]
  • [[Kerjasama strategik dengan agensi kerajaan, institusi pengajian tinggi (UIA, USIM) dan NGO.||Strategic partnerships with government agencies, universities (UIA, USIM) and NGOs.]]
