[[Inisiatif semasa||Current initiative]]

Taman Agro ARISMA

[[Projek pertanian sosial serta pembangunan terapi dan kemahiran hijau untuk belia autisme.||A social-agriculture project building therapy and green skills for autistic youth.]]

[[Daripada projek rintis, ke Taman Agro 2.0||From pilot project to Taman Agro 2.0]]

[[Pada 2018, projek rintis Taman Agro ARISMA dilaksanakan melalui tajaan CSR ANGKASA. Pelatih autisme mendapat latihan praktikal menanam secara hidroponik dan fertigasi.||In 2018 the Taman Agro ARISMA pilot ran with CSR sponsorship from ANGKASA. Autistic trainees gained hands-on training in hydroponic and fertigation farming.]]

[[Taman Agro ARISMA 2.0 dirancang sebagai hab agro-vokasional dan kehidupan berdikari.||Taman Agro ARISMA 2.0 is planned as an agro-vocational and independent-living hub.]]

[[Apa yang dirancang||What is planned]]

[[Latihan||Training]]

[[Latihan pertanian moden dan pemprosesan hasil, dengan sasaran 20 pelatih belia autisme dan keluarga mereka.||Modern farming and produce-processing training, targeting 20 autistic youth trainees and their families.]]

[[Mikro-pendapatan||Micro-income]]

[[Elaun bulanan kepada pelatih hasil jualan produk agro-makanan seperti jus dan sayuran segar.||A monthly stipend for trainees from sales of agro-food products such as juice and fresh vegetables.]]

[[Matlamat Pembangunan Mampan||Sustainable Development Goals]]

[[Selaras dengan SDG 4 (Pendidikan Berkualiti), SDG 8 (Pekerjaan Layak & Pertumbuhan Ekonomi) dan SDG 10 (Mengurangkan Ketidaksamaan).||Aligned with SDG 4 (Quality Education), SDG 8 (Decent Work & Economic Growth) and SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities).]]

[[Butiran projek dikemas kini dari masa ke masa apabila perancangan dimuktamadkan.||Project details are updated as planning is finalised.]]
