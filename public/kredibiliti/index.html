ALPA

Tadbir urus di bawah Lembaga Pemegang Amanah (Board of Trustees).Governed by a Board of Trustees.

Dana Yayasan diurus oleh Ahli Lembaga Pemegang Amanah (ALPA). Mengikut syarat kelulusan LHDN, pengasas dan pemegang amanah tidak boleh menerima saraan atau manfaat daripada Yayasan, dan tidak menggunakan kedudukan mereka untuk kepentingan tertentu.The Foundation’s funds are managed by its trustees. Under the LHDN approval conditions, founders and trustees may not receive remuneration or benefits from the Foundation, and may not use their position for personal interest.

Potongan cukai LHDN, subseksyen 44(6)LHDN tax deduction, subsection 44(6)

Sumbangan tunai kepada ARISMA Foundation layak menerima resit rasmi untuk tuntutan potongan cukai.Cash donations to ARISMA Foundation qualify for an official receipt for a tax-deduction claim.

No. rujukan LHDNLHDN reference
LHDN.AG.600-16/1/108.8403
Tempoh kuat kuasaValid
1 Julai 2024 hingga 31 Disember 20271 July 2024 to 31 December 2027
Pengeluar resitReceipt issuer
Hanya ARISMA Foundation. Akademi ARISMA / WIBARISMA Sdn. Bhd. tidak mengeluarkan resit 44(6).Only ARISMA Foundation. ARISMA Academy / WIBARISMA Sdn. Bhd. does not issue 44(6) receipts.

Panduan mendapatkan resitHow to get your receipt

  1. Sediakan maklumatPrepare your details

    Individu: nama, no. kad pengenalan / pasport dan alamat penuh. Syarikat: nama, no. pendaftaran dan alamat.Individuals: name, IC / passport number and full address. Companies: name, registration number and address.

  2. SumbangGive

    Melalui borang sumbangan dalam talian, atau pindahan bank dan e-mel bukti kepada kami.Through the online donation form, or by bank transfer and emailing us the slip.

  3. Terima resitReceive your receipt

    Resit rasmi dihantar ke e-mel anda, lengkap dengan nombor siri dan catatan LHDN.Your official receipt is emailed to you, with a serial number and the LHDN note.

Kenapa maklumat saya diperlukan?Why do you need my details?

LHDN tidak membenarkan resit dikeluarkan kepada penderma yang tidak memberikan maklumat lengkap: nama, no. pengenalan / pendaftaran dan alamat.LHDN does not allow a receipt to be issued to a donor who does not provide complete details: name, ID / registration number and address.

Adakah ada had potongan?Is there a deduction limit?

Potongan tertakluk kepada had dan peraturan di bawah Akta Cukai Pendapatan 1967. Sila rujuk penasihat cukai atau LHDN untuk keadaan anda.Deductions are subject to the limits and rules under the Income Tax Act 1967. Please consult your tax adviser or LHDN for your situation.

Boleh saya minta e-Invois?Can I request an e-Invoice?

Boleh. Hubungi kami dengan butiran penderma dan kami akan menguruskannya mengikut garis panduan e-Invois LHDN.Yes. Contact us with your donor details and we will handle it in line with LHDN’s e-Invoice guidelines.

Bagaimana dengan penajaan yang disertai manfaat?What about sponsorship that comes with benefits?

Penajaan yang disertai manfaat (iklan, hak penamaan) dilayan berbeza daripada sumbangan tulen. Butiran dipersetujui semasa perbincangan penajaan.Sponsorship with benefits (advertising, naming rights) is treated differently from a pure donation. Details are agreed during sponsorship discussions.
