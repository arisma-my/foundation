Siapa KamiAbout Us

ARISMA Foundation ialah badan amanah berdaftar yang bekerja untuk memperkasa komuniti autisme dan golongan memerlukan melalui inisiatif berkelanjutan.ARISMA Foundation is a registered trust working to empower the autism community and those in need through sustainable initiatives.

  • Kenapa kami wujudWhy we exist
  • Visi & MisiVision & Mission
  • ObjektifObjectives
  • Tadbir urusGovernance

Anak membesar. Ibu bapa menua.Children grow up. Parents grow old.

Ditubuhkan pada 2014, ARISMA Foundation wujud supaya remaja dan dewasa autisme tidak dilupakan selepas zaman sekolah, dan keluarga mereka tidak memikulnya seorang diri.Founded in 2014, ARISMA Foundation exists so that autistic young people and adults are not forgotten once school ends, and their families do not carry it alone.

  • Anak membesar menjadi dewasaChildren become adults

    Apabila ibu bapa makin uzur atau meninggal dunia, perlu ada orang yang terus mengambil berat dan melindungi mereka.As parents grow frail or pass away, someone must keep caring for and protecting them.

  • Sokongan untuk dewasa masih kurangSupport for adults is scarce

    Kebanyakan perkhidmatan autisme tertumpu kepada kanak-kanak. Selepas tamat sekolah, pilihan menjadi jauh lebih sedikit.Most autism services focus on children. After school, the options become far fewer.

  • Jawapan kamiOur response

    Kesedaran awam, sokongan sosial dan pembangunan inklusif, bersama komuniti, agensi dan rakan korporat.Public awareness, social support and inclusive development, together with communities, agencies and corporate partners.

Visi & MisiVision & Mission

VisiVision

Menjadi peneraju utama dalam memperkasa sosioekonomi dan kebajikan komuniti autisme serta golongan memerlukan.To be a leading force in empowering the socio-economic wellbeing and welfare of the autism community and those in need.

MisiMission

Membangunkan program kesedaran, program latihan, serta jaringan sokongan holistik berasaskan nilai-nilai murni.Developing awareness programmes, training programmes and a holistic support network built on sound values.

Objektif utamaKey objectives

  1. Platform sokongan komunitiCommunity support platform

    Menyediakan platform sokongan komuniti dan pembangunan modal insan.Providing a community support platform and human-capital development.

  2. Pengurusan dana yang telusTransparent fund management

    Menguruskan dana infaq dan kebajikan secara telus, beretika dan berimpak tinggi.Managing giving and welfare funds transparently, ethically and with high impact.

  3. Kerjasama strategikStrategic partnerships

    Menjalin kerjasama bersama agensi kerajaan, institusi pengajian tinggi (UIA, USIM) dan NGO.Building partnerships with government agencies, universities (UIA, USIM) and NGOs.

Tadbir urusGovernance

Dana Yayasan diurus oleh lembaga pemegang amanah yang diluluskan oleh Menteri di Jabatan Perdana Menteri, mengikut Surat Ikatan Amanah bertarikh 10 Julai 2014.The Foundation’s funds are managed by a board of trustees approved by the Minister in the Prime Minister’s Department, under a Trust Deed dated 10 July 2014.
